★ Celebrating 50 Years ★

1976 to 2026  •  Half a century of Christmas Eve groceries, kindness, and hope

Police Officer's Christmas Angels, Inc. (POCA, Inc.)

A non-profit 501(c) corporation • AKA Corrigan’s Christmas Grocery Event • rncj@cccranch.org

To all my Elves,

Ho! Ho! Ho! This Christmas Eve marks something truly special: our 50th Anniversary. Since 1976, the Corrigan Family and Friends have gathered before sunrise on Christmas Eve to deliver groceries to families in need, identified by the Corpus Christi Police Department. What began as a small act of kindness has grown into a cherished community tradition, and every one of you is part of that story.

Last year, together, we delivered to 135 families. For our golden anniversary we would love to match that number and reach even more homes. Please help us celebrate 50 years by giving generously and by inviting new elves to join the team.

A gift of $200 or more makes a real difference on a family’s Christmas table. Please let us know if we can count on you, then choose whichever way to give is easiest below.

★ Christmas Eve Morning ★

Thursday, December 24, 2026

--Days
--Hours
--Minutes
--Seconds

  1. 7:00 AM

    Please arrive early

    Arrive at Windsor Park Elementary School, 4525 South Alameda Street, by about 7:00 am to get settled and find your team.

  2. 7:30 AM

    Team Meeting and Staging

    We officially meet at 7:30 am at Windsor Park Elementary. Police leaders, Team Captains, and Group Leaders will have large numbers on their windshields so you can find your group. We will also take a group photo at 7:30 am to commemorate our 50th anniversary, so please be on time!

  3. Next

    Load the groceries

    Teams travel together to the HEB east parking lot at Robert Drive and Alameda Street, Corpus Christi, to load vehicles.

  4. Deliver

    Spread the Christmas joy

    Grocery deliveries go out as a team from the HEB east parking lot to each family on our list. Deliveries are typically concluded within 2 to 3 hours.

Please bring large vehicles or trucks so your team has plenty of room to load grocery boxes.

Toys are welcome too! In recent years, many of our elves have also brought small toys for children of all ages to deliver along with the groceries. We happily leave that to your discretion, and the smiles are unforgettable.
